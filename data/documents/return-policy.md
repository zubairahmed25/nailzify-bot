# Return Policy

## Money-Back Guarantee

We want you to be absolutely in love with your Nailzify nails! Nailzify offers a
14-day money-back guarantee. We want you to love our products, but understand if
you need a different style. A $6 return label fee will be charged for refunds
back to the original form of payment, or get a free return label if you select
store credit.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If you received a damaged or defective item please contact us within 14 days of
receiving your order.

## International Orders

International orders must be returned at the expense of the customer. Please
email customer support for assistance.
