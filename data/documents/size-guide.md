# Size Guide for Handmade Nails

Nailzify press-on nail sets come in four set sizes: XS, S, M and L. Each set
contains nails sized for every finger, so you choose one set size rather than
a size per nail.

## Size Chart

All sizes are in millimetres (mm). Each column is the width of that finger's
nail in the set.

| Set size | Thumb | Index | Middle | Ring | Little |
| -------- | ----- | ----- | ------ | ---- | ------ |
| XS       | 14    | 10    | 11     | 10   | 8      |
| S        | 15    | 11    | 12     | 11   | 9      |
| M        | 16    | 12    | 13     | 12   | 10     |
| L        | 17    | 13    | 14     | 13   | 11     |

## How To Measure

Please measure the widest part of your nail bed.

Use a flexible tape measure or a strip of paper laid across the nail at its
widest point, then read the measurement in millimetres.

## Choosing Your Size

- Choose the size of nail set that best matches the majority of your fingernails.
- Go with the larger size if you're uncertain between two options, or if you have
  wider, flatter nail shapes.
- We always recommend sizing up 1–2mm — you can easily file down to fit, but you
  can't make them longer.
